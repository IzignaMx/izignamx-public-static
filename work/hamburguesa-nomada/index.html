Hamburguesa Nómada

A portable event experience built around QR access

A responsive web experience that packages event information and participation flows into a lightweight, installable interface.

Visit the live experience Inspect the public evidence

01

Context and problem

Event information needs to remain immediate and legible while people move between physical spaces, devices, and network conditions.

02

Decisions

  • Treat the QR entry point as the beginning of the product journey.
  • Keep essential event content available in a compact responsive hierarchy.
  • Use static delivery and PWA capabilities to reduce operational complexity.

03

Inspectable delivery

  • Responsive event information architecture
  • QR-ready public entry points
  • Installable progressive web application behavior

Evidence boundary

Show what can be inspected.

The live experience, public repository, and portfolio record verify the published product and implementation. No attendance or conversion result is claimed.

What we learned

In a physical event, speed and orientation matter more than ornamental interface density.
