NutriChilango

A public data experience for comparing local food context

A map-led information product that brings structured comparisons and practical context into an accessible static experience.

Explore the live product Inspect the public evidence

01

Context and problem

Public information loses value when people cannot compare it quickly or understand where it applies in their daily context.

02

Decisions

  • Shape source data into understandable comparisons before designing the interface.
  • Use maps as orientation rather than decoration.
  • Automate static updates so the public experience remains simple to serve.

03

Inspectable delivery

  • Map-led exploration and comparison views
  • Responsive editorial explanations
  • A scheduled static-data publishing workflow

Evidence boundary

Show what can be inspected.

The live experience, public repository, and portfolio record verify the product and documented delivery approach. No health outcome or usage metric is claimed.

What we learned

Data becomes useful when comparison, provenance, and geographic context are visible together.
