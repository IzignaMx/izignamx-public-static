01 / OBSERVE

What changes when you interact

The coordinates combine oscillations with relative frequencies 2:3:5. A marker travels around a closed loop. Amplitude changes its height, phase changes its configuration and filaments reveal its structure from different angles.

02 / INTERPRET

Geometry, not a scientific claim

A parametric study inspired by Lissajous curves extended to three dimensions. Not planetary orbits, electrons or measured particle trajectories. Proportions are design choices without physical units.

03 / APPLY

From exploration to your product

A way to reveal parameter relationships in configurators or data stories. Use a keyboard, pause the journey or change perspective. The scene retains a static alternative and access to the portfolio.
