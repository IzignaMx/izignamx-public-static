Before redesigning your ecommerce, find the friction.

Separate problems of clarity, trust, navigation and contact from changes that only refresh the appearance.

Define the decision you are helping someone make

Before choosing typography or animation, name the task the page should support. It might be comparing a solution, finding a compatible product, understanding delivery or requesting a quotation. If the team cannot name that task, a redesign can change many things without resolving a clear need.

Review an actual page against that objective and note which information appears first. Merely including information somewhere is not enough. An important condition discovered only after starting a conversation is still friction. Our proposed order is clarity before the number of interface elements.

Replace pressure with information

Trust does not require invented countdowns or unverifiable testimonials. Prefer a concrete account of scope, delivery conditions and how questions are handled. When a condition depends on the project, say so and explain how it will be decided.

Published cases should distinguish delivered work from measured results. A screenshot demonstrates an interface, not an increase in sales. A repository makes implementation inspectable, not future maintenance guaranteed. That distinction helps a commercial conversation start with realistic expectations.

Test the journey beyond the homepage

Follow an entry page through to the final action on a small screen. Check that names, choices, button labels and errors remain understandable. A polished header cannot compensate for clipped form labels or conditions that are hard to find.

Use a keyboard as well. Labels should identify controls and errors should explain corrections. Do not assume everyone uses a mouse, sees a border color or retains context after a failure. W3C guidance on forms provides a practical reference for those checks.

Decide what counts as a result

Opening WhatsApp, preparing a message and having a qualified project conversation are different events. Combining them produces attractive numbers but makes actual progress difficult to understand. Track the distinction without storing information that is not needed.

Choose one hypothesis per iteration, such as presenting a condition before a form or asking for one fewer item at the start. Decide how the outcome will be compared and which side effects matter. This is a review method, not a promise of increased conversion.

Turn findings into a short decision list

Order findings by their impact on the task, risk and effort. Start with actions that cannot be completed, misleading information and accessibility barriers. Then refine hierarchy and content. Leave ornamental adjustments until the structure already helps people decide.

To start a review with IzignaMx, share the page, the action you expect a visitor to take and the point where friction appears. A technical solution is not required. Useful context helps distinguish a targeted improvement from a deeper investigation or a broader redesign.
