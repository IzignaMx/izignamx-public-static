Orient someone who is already moving

Someone scanning a QR code at an event arrives with a different context from a desktop visitor. They need to recognize where they are, find relevant information and understand what to do next.

This case treats the QR entry point as the beginning of the product rather than a link added at the end. Information hierarchy and responsive behavior connect the physical and digital experience.

Information before ornament

The published implementation combines a compact information structure, static delivery and progressive web application capabilities. The intention is clear orientation without unnecessary operational complexity.

  • Design from the QR entry point.
  • Present essential event information in a readable hierarchy.
  • Offer installation without making it a requirement.

A portable, inspectable journey

The documented delivery includes responsive event information, QR-ready entry points and installable PWA behavior. The screenshot comes from an earlier review of the product rather than generated promotional artwork.

Installation is not a guarantee of offline availability, and we do not claim every part of the journey works on every connection. Those behaviors require device-specific validation. For a similar project, deciding what must work under limited connectivity should be an early scope decision rather than a launch surprise.

What supports this case

The case documents the published experience and its implementation. It does not claim increased attendance, sales or conversion.
