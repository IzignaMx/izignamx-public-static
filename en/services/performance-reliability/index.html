BEFORE A PROPOSAL

What shapes the investment.

Budget follows scope, dependencies and delivery criteria. These are the variables we would review with you.

How to prepare your brief
  • Journeys, devices and languages.
  • Access to code, observability and test environments.
  • Reproduction complexity and integrations.
  • Depth of review and implementation.

A UX or automated review is not a penetration test or accessibility certification. Laboratory data does not replace real-user measurements.

Before taking the next step.

Is a Lighthouse score enough?

We would not use it alone. The environment, journeys and symptoms matter, as does distinguishing laboratory and field information.

Does a review include every fix?

Diagnosis and implementation can be separate stages. The proposal should state what is delivered, corrected or recommended.

Are tests run with production data?

We prefer controlled environments and data. Tests affecting production need agreed scope, permission and recovery measures.
