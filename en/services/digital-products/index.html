BEFORE A PROPOSAL

What shapes the investment.

Budget follows scope, dependencies and delivery criteria. These are the variables we would review with you.

How to prepare your brief
  • Business rules and roles or permissions.
  • API and data quality and availability.
  • Migrations, compatibility and technical debt.
  • Operations, human review and recovery.

AI is not added by default. Accounts, licences, API costs and support are agreed separately. A prototype is not presented as production-ready.

Before taking the next step.

Can it work without AI?

Yes. When conventional rules and workflows solve the problem, they are our starting point. AI needs a purpose and clear limits.

Can you continue another team’s code?

We can assess it before proposing changes. Authorized access, context and ownership and maintenance constraints are necessary.

What remains under my control?

The proposal should clarify code, accounts, data, documentation and handoff permissions. Third-party component licences keep their own terms.
