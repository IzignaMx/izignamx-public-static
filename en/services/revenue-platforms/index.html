BEFORE A PROPOSAL

What shapes the investment.

Budget follows scope, dependencies and delivery criteria. These are the variables we would review with you.

How to prepare your brief
  • Template complexity, not just page count.
  • Content, catalogue and language readiness.
  • Payments, stock and existing integrations.
  • URL migration and verification scope.

Advertising, licences, full copywriting, maintenance and third-party fees are defined separately. We do not promise sales or search rankings.

Before taking the next step.

Does the whole website need replacing?

Not necessarily. We first separate what works, what blocks the journey and what can improve without a full rebuild.

Can you work with an existing store?

We can assess the platform, catalogue and integrations. Permissions, constraints and current condition determine the solution, not a default migration.

What is needed for a proposal?

The current URL, your offer, the action you need and an example of friction. Indicate which content is ready and what remains to be created.
