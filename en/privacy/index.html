How this website works

This website is published as static files on GitHub Pages under izignamx.com, with Cloudflare delivering the domain. Hosting and network providers may process requests and technical logs under their own terms. A Higgsfield account is not required to browse.

The form prepares the message in your browser. You choose to open WhatsApp or your email application and send it. The form does not submit its contents to this website’s server. Do not include sensitive information in the first contact.

Data processing and external services

This static website minimizes data collection and uses Cloudflare Browser Insights/Web Analytics to understand aggregate visits and browser performance. Direct contact channels are handled by their respective providers.

  • Cloudflare may process the page path, referrer, a random page-load identifier, navigation and paint timings, memory information, and Core Web Vitals such as FCP, LCP, CLS, TTFB, and INP.
  • Cloudflare states that this beacon does not use cookies or browser storage, does not log URL query strings, and does not fingerprint visitors. The source IP reaches Cloudflare and is discarded at the nearest data center rather than stored in its Web Analytics core database or logs.
  • Unsampled beacon records are retained for seven days, then reduced and aggregated for longer-term storage; dashboard data is generally available for about six months. Processing may occur across Cloudflare's global network.
  • Email and WhatsApp open third-party services that apply their own privacy terms. Do not send sensitive information through initial contact.
  • No personal information is sold.
  • Privacy questions can be sent to edgar@izignamx.com .

Display preferences

Your choice between direct view and the immersive experience is stored locally under izigna-view. This preference does not store names, email addresses or form contents.

Commercial interactions generate local events without form fields. This implementation has no analytics destination connected to those events. This does not exclude technical processing by the hosting provider.

Privacy inquiries

You can write to edgar@izignamx.com with privacy questions. External contact channels are subject to their respective providers’ terms.
